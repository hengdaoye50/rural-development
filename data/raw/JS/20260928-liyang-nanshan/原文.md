摘要
摘要:
本文探讨了新质生产力导向下乡村片区规划的逻辑重构，以江苏省溧阳市南山和美乡村片区为案例，分析了传统乡村规划面临的挑战及新质生产力对乡村发展的推动作用。研究构建了新质生产力与乡村片区化规划的深度耦合机制，包括技术创新驱动要素协同、制度创新激活空间重构与生态创新赋能价值转化。通过技术革命、制度创新与生态创新，南山和美乡村片区实现了从“空间设计”向“发展动能设计”的转型，有效提升了全要素生产率与多元价值均衡。然而，片区在区域协同治理、特色竞争力培育及产业能级跃迁等方面仍存在挑战。本文的研究为乡村规划转型提供了新视角，对推动乡村振兴与城乡融合发展具有重要意义。
Abstract:
This paper explores the logical restructuring of rural area planning under the guidance of new quality productive forces, using Liyang's Nanshan Harmonious and Beautiful Countryside as a case study. It analyzes the challenges facing traditional rural planning and the catalytic role of new quality productive forces in driving rural development. The research constructs a deep-coupling mechanism between new quality productive forces and rural area-based planning, encompassing technological innovation-driven factor synergy, institutional innovation activating spatial restructuring, and ecological innovation enabling value conversion. Through technological revolution, institutional innovation, and ecological innovation, the Nanshan Harmonious and Beautiful Countryside has achieved a transformation from 'spatial design' to 'development momentum design', effectively enhancing Total Factor Productivity and balancing multiple values. However, challenges remain in areas such as regional collaborative governance, cultivation of distinctive competitiveness, and industrial upgrading leaps. This study provides a new perspective for rural planning transformation and holds significant importance for advancing rural revitalization and integrated urban-rural development.
